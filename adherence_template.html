BDR / SDR Salesloft cadence execution by rep, team, and cadence. Grouped by cadence assignee.

Adherence detail

Methodology & Salesloft API source
Live v1. Grouped by cadence ASSIGNEE (the rep working the step), team from the rep's Salesloft group. Scope is usage-based: every cadence your XDRs have open work in (not name-filtered). Refreshed daily.

Two honest caveats: (1) Cadence Completion % and Premature Removal % are computed over each cadence's full membership history, so they are lifetime rates, not windowed. (2) Avg Days Late can be very large where people sit in a cadence with a long-overdue step; read it alongside the overdue count.

Coming in v2: On-Time % (the actions-ledger is now accumulating daily and will compute completed-on-time vs late over the next few weeks), plus Owner=Assignee %, throughput, and call/email step completion.
