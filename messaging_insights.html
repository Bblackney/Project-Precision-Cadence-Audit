2

BDR-Strategic replies 2.4x better than BDR-Velocity, and 15% of it is which themes they run

BDR-Strategic runs at 1.54% and BDR-Velocity at 0.65%. The concrete difference in the copy: BDR-Strategic puts 37% of its volume into peer_proof against 9% for BDR-Velocity; BDR-Velocity puts 28% of its volume into risk_trust, which replies at 0.32% for them, while BDR-Strategic does not use it at all. Re-weighting BDR-Velocity to BDR-Strategic's theme mix would take it to 0.82%, which is about 15% of the gap. The rest is audience or execution, not which themes are used.

Do: Move BDR-Velocity's risk_trust volume (4,093 sends) into peer_proof. That closes roughly 15% of the gap. Investigate the remaining 85% separately, it is not a copy problem.
