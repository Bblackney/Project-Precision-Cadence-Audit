SDR reply rate falls 53% from the first two steps to step 7 and beyond

Early steps reply at 6.57%, late steps at 3.08%, with no interval overlap. Early steps are mostly inbound_ack (6.57%). At late steps no theme clears the volume and cadence-diversity bar, so there is no copy swap to recommend: the decay looks structural, not a framing choice.

2

BDR-Strategic replies 2.4x better than BDR-Velocity, and 24% of it is which themes they run

BDR-Strategic runs at 1.58% and BDR-Velocity at 0.67%. The concrete difference in the copy: BDR-Strategic puts 40% of its volume into peer_proof against 7% for BDR-Velocity; BDR-Velocity puts 28% of its volume into risk_trust, which replies at 0.24% for them, while BDR-Strategic does not use it at all. Re-weighting BDR-Velocity to BDR-Strategic's theme mix would take it to 0.87%, which is about 24% of the gap. The rest is audience or execution, not which themes are used.

Do: Move BDR-Velocity's risk_trust volume (2,535 sends) into peer_proof. That closes roughly 24% of the gap. Investigate the remaining 76% separately, it is not a copy problem.
